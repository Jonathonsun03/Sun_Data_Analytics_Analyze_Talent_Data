PRIMARY CODE: NARRATIVE_SERIALIZATION

Definition:
Content explicitly positioned as part of an ongoing sequence, arc, season, or numbered series.

Inclusion Criteria:
- Episode numbers, part markers, season language, chapter structure, "finale", "arc".

Exclusion Criteria:
- One-off uploads with no sequential framing.
- "Day X" alone does NOT constitute narrative serialization.
- Advent calendar-style counting alone is insufficient.

Mapping Guidance:
- Keep topic tied to the core activity.
- Add tags such as `episode`, `part`, `season`, `series_arc`, `finale`.
