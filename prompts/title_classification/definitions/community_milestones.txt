PRIMARY CODE: COMMUNITY_MILESTONES

Definition:
Content tied to audience/community milestones, anniversaries, debuts, or special events.

Inclusion Criteria:
- Debut, anniversary, celebration, subscriber/follower milestones, thank-you specials.
- Milestone tokens such as: birthday, 10k, 100k, sub goal, donothon.

Exclusion Criteria:
- Routine streams with generic excitement but no milestone evidence.

Mapping Guidance:
- Prefer topic = community_milestone when the title is primarily a debut, model debut, birthday, anniversary, subathon wrap-up, subscriber milestone, thank-you event, membership launch, or community celebration.
- Co-occurs with other primaries; do not force topic changes if another domain is dominant, such as a named game, karaoke/cover, or explicit ASMR format.
- Add tags such as `debut`, `anniversary`, `milestone`, `celebration`.
