Calculation Notes
  • Date filters apply to analytics snapshot dates, matching the creator dashboard.
  • Each talent contributes only its latest snapshot within the selected range.
  • Total views and video-page subscription events are cumulative values at those snapshots.
  • Total content hours sum the catalogued duration of each distinct video or stream; the displayed day conversion is a scale reference, not elapsed calendar time or audience watch time.
  • Watch hours use cumulative estimated minutes watched from YouTube Analytics.
  • Average view duration is aggregate watch seconds divided by total views.
  • Average view percentage is weighted by views rather than averaged equally across releases.
  • Monthly and weekday panels group those distinct videos by publication date.
  • Total views are not unique viewers or unique reach. Unsupported audience fields remain explicitly unavailable.
  • Company membership is maintained in config/dashboard/company_talents.csv.
